# Technology Impact QA

## QA criteria

Attack a shared premise that could survive competent production and review. Use a small number of credible probes tied to the accepted outcome and actual operating conditions. These are hypotheses until exercised or traced; they do not authorize unsafe experiments.

- **Benefit inversion:** assume the sponsor's metric improves while the affected operator's task gets harder. Trace where work, cost or accountability went. Could all local measures pass while the intended benefit fails?
- **Meaning laundering:** hold the label constant and substitute a weaker producer meaning, such as request accepted for execution stopped. Can downstream consumers still report verified success while the accepted guarantee is false?
- **Nominal control:** trace the real override, revocation or exit path with a stale session, unavailable provider or partial completion. Does control survive the condition in which it is needed, where that control is promised?
- **Capability composition:** combine two allowed capabilities or a benign reuse with retained data. Can the combination enable a materially different use, authority transfer or exposure that neither component's review considered?
- **Assumption drift:** change one load-bearing premise, such as account binding, retention duration or affected population. Does reused analysis falsely authorize the changed situation?
- **Paper mitigation:** remove the prose saying a risk is handled. Find the enforcing boundary and its evidence. Challenge a plan or test that validates the same mistaken assumption as the implementation.

Preserve accepted scope and genuine independent work. A hypothetical stronger guarantee is advisory, not an automatic blocker. Record the causal chain, affected claim, observed evidence, residual uncertainty and owner. Self-review using this file is not independent QA, and a proposed probe is not a passing result.
