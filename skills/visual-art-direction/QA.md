# Visual art-direction confidence challenges

Use these probes when polished work may conceal a weak direction. They do not establish new product requirements.

- Replace unusually short labels and sparse sample data with representative long, dense, empty, or mixed-state content. Does the composition still serve the task?
- Compare the whole task with the showcase screenshot. Look for weak error/recovery states, disconnected visual grammar, or ornament that impedes repeated work.
- Challenge a declared winner selected from differently polished alternatives or different content. Could presentation effort explain the preference?
- Check whether shared tokens conceal loss of platform fit, or whether surface adaptation loses important state, vocabulary, or progress.
- Remove color, custom glyphs, or motion where relevant and inspect whether meaning survives. Do not infer accessible behavior solely from that visual check.
- Ask whether a generic-output diagnosis would also condemn a strong conventional interface. Identify the actual mismatch before requesting more novelty.
- Check evidence provenance and limits: stale renders, idealized data, mock capabilities, inferred preference, and static images presented as timing or interaction proof.

Record the conditions actually examined. These probes can expose false confidence; their presence in a document is not evidence that they ran.
