# Visual art-direction assessment

Assess the produced direction and its evidence against the accepted task. Do not substitute the reviewer's favorite style.

- The intended character is tied to audience, task, content, and setting and translated into consequential visual decisions.
- References are actually inspected or identified as unavailable, attributed, and analyzed for transferable mechanisms rather than copied as a whole style.
- When exploration was needed, alternatives differ in meaningful composition, hierarchy, density, interaction, or character. A bounded refinement need not have alternatives.
- Composition, typography, spacing, density, imagery, color, and motion form a coherent language at relevant states and sizes. A token inventory alone does not demonstrate craft.
- Visual expression preserves readable information, semantic states, usable controls, and applicable accessibility and platform constraints.
- Supported surfaces preserve shared meaning while adapting layout and interaction; a terminal or spoken experience is not judged by GUI ornament.
- The review distinguishes observed rendered quality from predicted user response, preference, interaction behavior, and implementation capability.
- Generic-output findings identify a concrete mismatch; no stylistic trope is automatically forbidden, and novelty is not a quota.
- The handoff preserves accepted visual intent, examples, constraints, open decisions, and actual evidence without requiring unnecessary new artifacts.

Treat aesthetic judgments as contextual professional assessments. A beautiful rendering cannot cancel a serious task or accessibility defect. Disagreement over preference is not automatically nonconformance.
