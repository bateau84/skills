# visual-interface-design Reviewer Assessment

Domain-specific review guidance for any agent performing assessment. It does not establish product decisions or prove correctness by itself.

## Review criteria

Reviewer asks whether visual treatment communicates the accepted information/interaction model and expresses the intended product character with coherent craft:

1. **Hierarchy/reading order** — priority can be inferred from type, spacing, grouping, position, emphasis, and density rather than subjective claims such as “stands out.”
2. **Semantic state distinction** — status, severity, selection, disabled/focus/error/success and other meaningful states are visually distinguishable and have non-color redundancy where meaning would otherwise be lost.
3. **Legibility/density** — type roles, line length, spacing, contrast, and information density fit the content and supported viewport/scale conditions. Judge rendered results, not a mandatory pixel step, monospace family, or spacing multiple.
4. **Design-system coherence** — tokens, typography, iconography, spacing, component visual language, themes, and reasoned exceptions form a system. Optical adjustments may improve coherence; unmotivated variation may damage it.
5. **Affordance** — actionable vs static content is visually understandable without contradicting interaction semantics.
6. **Responsive/theme behavior** — hierarchy and meaning survive narrow layouts, zoom/scaling, dark/high-contrast themes where supported.
7. **Motion purpose/control** — motion supports feedback, orientation, or accepted character without blocking routine work or competing with attention. Reduced-motion and pause/update behavior follow the applicable criteria; a fixed duration alone proves neither accessibility nor quality.
8. **Decorative freedom** — decorative color/shape is allowed; it becomes a defect only when it obscures hierarchy, accessibility, brand/system coherence, or semantic meaning.
9. **Boundary discipline** — aesthetics do not invent product behavior or overwrite accepted interaction/information architecture.
10. **Craft and evidence** — rendered composition, type, rhythm, imagery, and detail support the accepted direction across the inspected states. Identify concrete observations and evidence limits. Expert critique is legitimate; it does not prove user preference or task success. A bounded polish request need not produce novel alternatives.
