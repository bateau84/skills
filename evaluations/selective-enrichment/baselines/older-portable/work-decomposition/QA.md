# Work Decomposition — Older Portable QA

The original assessment's adversarial subsection, separated without changing its methodological intent:

Attack over-fragmentation and semantic smearing. Try merging adjacent groups and splitting mixed groups; a boundary should survive only when removing it creates a real execution, safety, ownership, or evidence problem.

Lost guarantees, unsafe shared-state execution, hidden foundation/integration work, orphaned acceptance proof, or implementation forced to decide product/architecture meaning are substantive defects. Do not optimize task count.

A reader aid does not create plan authority. Separate harmless summary/editorial differences from misleading scope/ownership/ordering/verification or defects in the executable plan itself; follow the underlying defect rather than manufacturing severity from wording alone.
